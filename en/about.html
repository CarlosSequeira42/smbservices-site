About Us

Resilience isn't a product. It's a commitment.

SMB Services was founded with a specific focus: making sure the data of the companies we serve survives any scenario — hardware failure, human error or a ransomware attack.

Instead of selling "yet another backup," we help each client answer a simple question: if something goes wrong today, can I actually recover my data in time? Most companies only find out the answer during a real crisis — our job is to make sure they find out before that, with time to fix it.
