Backup & Recovery · November 14, 2026

You restore the backup. The system is still down. Has this happened to you?

It sounds contradictory, but it's more common than you'd think: the backup comes back, but the business is still stuck — because the network configuration, user access, and the right order to bring everything back online are still missing.

Commvault just launched a tool to solve exactly that: Recovery Studio. Instead of just bringing the data back, it orchestrates the entire recovery — data, identity, network, configuration — in a single automated flow, using predefined runbooks.

The number Commvault itself published: today, on average, a business takes 28 days to resume even minimal operations after a serious incident. Nearly a month in the dark.

One detail worth highlighting: the tool doesn't use AI to "guess" what to do. It simply follows a runbook that the business itself needs to have designed in advance — which reinforces something we keep saying here: a backup tool doesn't replace recovery planning. That work has to happen before the incident, not during it.

The tool enters early access in December 2026, with general availability expected in the following months.
