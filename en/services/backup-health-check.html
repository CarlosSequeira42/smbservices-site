Backup Health Check

What it is

A complete diagnostic of your current backup environment: we check whether backups are actually being taken, whether they're restorable, and whether they meet the recovery deadlines your business needs.

Who it's for

Companies that already have a backup solution running, but have never validated it independently — or that have a feeling it "must be working," without real confirmation.

What's included

  • A complete survey of existing backup policies and jobs
  • Restore tests on real data samples
  • Detection of silent failures (jobs that "pass" but don't protect what they should)
  • A report with the risks found and a prioritized remediation plan
