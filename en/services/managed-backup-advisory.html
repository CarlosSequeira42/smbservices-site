Managed Backup Advisory

What it is

Ongoing, specialized monitoring of the client's backup strategy — we monitor, adjust and advise on a recurring basis, rather than as a one-off project.

Who it's for

Companies that don't have an internal team dedicated to backup/data recovery, or that prefer having a trusted specialist tracking this long-term, rather than only dealing with it when a problem comes up.

What's included

  • Periodic monitoring of backup health
  • Policy adjustments as the client's environment grows or changes
  • Regular status updates (report + meeting)
  • Priority support in case of an incident
