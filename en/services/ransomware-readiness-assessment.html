Ransomware Readiness Assessment

What it is

A real test of your recovery capability after a ransomware attack — not just whether you have backups, but whether you can actually restore a clean, operational environment without paying a ransom.

Who it's for

Companies that take the risk of ransomware seriously (today the leading cause of prolonged downtime for SMBs) and want to confirm the recovery plan works before they need it.

What's included

  • Assessment of backup immutability and isolation (protection against malicious encryption/deletion)
  • Simulation of a post-attack recovery scenario
  • Verification of entry points and propagation paths that would affect backup
  • A documented response and recovery plan

(To be confirmed: whether the simulation is run in an environment isolated from the client's.)
