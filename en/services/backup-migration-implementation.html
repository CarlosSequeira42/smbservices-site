Backup Migration & Implementation

What it is

We implement a backup solution from scratch, or migrate from an old/inadequate solution to a robust platform (Commvault, Veeam), without interrupting current operations.

Who it's for

Companies without a structured backup setup, or that need to switch provider/technology due to limitations, cost, or lack of support from their current solution.

What's included

  • Design of a backup architecture suited to the client's environment
  • Migration of policies and history (where possible) from the previous solution
  • Implementation, configuration and validation testing
  • Documentation and training for the internal team
