Infrastructure Resilience Assessment

What it is

An assessment that goes beyond backup: we analyze your entire infrastructure's ability — servers, network, storage — to withstand a failure and keep running, or to recover within the time your business can tolerate.

Who it's for

Companies that need to know, with concrete numbers, how much downtime they'd face in a failure scenario — and what to do to reduce it.

What's included

  • Mapping of single points of failure across the infrastructure
  • Calculation of actual RTO (recovery time) and RPO (acceptable data loss) vs. desired targets
  • Assessment of existing redundancy and contingency plans
  • Recommendations prioritized by business impact
