FAQ

Frequently asked questions

I already have backup. Why do I need a Health Check?

Having a backup job configured doesn't guarantee the data is actually restorable. Many failures only show up at restore time — by which point it's already too late to fix. The Health Check tests this before you need it.

What's the difference between backup and resilience?

Backup is having a copy of the data. Resilience is the ability to get back up and running quickly after an incident — it involves infrastructure, processes and recovery time, not just the existence of a copy.

Do backups protect against ransomware?

Not always. If the backup isn't isolated/immutable, it can be encrypted or deleted in the same attack that affects the original data. The Ransomware Readiness Assessment specifically checks for this.
